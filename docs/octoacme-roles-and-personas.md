# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsors

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide strategic direction, secure funding, and help remove organizational barriers that could block delivery or adoption. They ensure the initiative continues to align with business goals and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic objectives and expected outcomes
- Sponsor prioritization decisions and trade-offs
- Support issue escalation and executive decision-making
- Align project outcomes with broader portfolio or business goals
- Champion adoption and stakeholder confidence across the organization

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work closely with Product Managers to confirm the business value and success measures for the initiative
- Partner with Project Managers to review milestones, risk exposure, and cross-functional dependencies
- Provide direction to leadership and functional teams when scope or resource constraints require executive intervention

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee or sponsor reviews
- Executive status updates and business decision checkpoints
- High-level approvals, escalations, and investment decisions

---

## Delivery Managers

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers coordinate execution across workstreams, dependencies, and teams. They help maintain momentum, surface issues early, and keep teams focused on outcomes while supporting stakeholder visibility.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate sprint or release cadence across teams and workstreams
- Track dependencies, blockers, and handoffs between groups
- Maintain delivery health indicators and escalation paths
- Support project planning and prioritization with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Facilitate operational alignment across engineering, operations, and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Project Managers to maintain plans, schedules, and reporting cadence
- Work with Developers and Product Managers to clarify priorities and sequencing
- Align with Sponsors and stakeholders to communicate delivery status, trade-offs, and risks

### Typical Communication
- Delivery reviews and dependency check-ins
- Cross-team release and milestone coordination
- Dependency and escalation updates for leadership or stakeholders

---

## Change Managers / Adoption Leads

### Role Summary
Change Managers help teams and stakeholders prepare for transitions, process changes, and new ways of working. They reduce adoption risk by making communication, training, and transition planning part of the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Assess stakeholder impact and readiness for change
- Coordinate communication plans for new processes, features, or operating models
- Support training, enablement, and feedback loops
- Identify adoption risks and mitigation actions
- Align change activities with rollout plans and business readiness milestones

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Product Managers to ensure customer and internal stakeholder needs are reflected in communications and training
- Work with Project Managers to sequence communications and adoption milestones with delivery plans
- Partner with Developers and Operations / Service Owners to support user readiness and post-launch transition activities

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder readiness assessments
- Training plans and adoption updates
- Change impact briefings and feedback review sessions

---

## Quality & Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Quality & Compliance Leads help ensure the delivered work meets required standards, acceptance criteria, and regulatory or operational obligations. They focus on quality, risk reduction, and predictable delivery outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define or validate quality expectations and acceptance criteria
- Review work against compliance requirements and standards
- Identify quality risks, test gaps, or control issues early in delivery
- Support continuous improvement and governance review cycles
- Partner with project stakeholders on remediation and prevention

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to define measurable quality gates and test coverage expectations
- Coordinate with Product Managers to confirm requirements, business rules, and user impact
- Support Project Managers and Sponsors by surfacing risk trends and quality issues that may affect timelines or business confidence

### Typical Communication
- Quality and compliance reviews
- Risk and remediation discussions
- Acceptance, validation, and control checkpoints

---

## Operations / Service Owners

### Role Summary
Operations or Service Owners ensure that an implemented solution or process can be supported after delivery. They define the ongoing operating model, support expectations, and handoff criteria for sustainable adoption.

### Responsibilities
- Own operational readiness and post-launch support model
- Define service ownership, support procedures, and operational metrics
- Participate in handoff planning for production readiness
- Monitor service health, backlog, and operational issues after launch
- Coordinate with delivery teams on support, maintenance, and improvements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Project Managers and Delivery Managers on implementation and transition plans
- Work with Developers to understand technical dependencies and support requirements
- Coordinate with Product Managers and Sponsors to ensure service outcomes are aligned with business expectations

### Typical Communication
- Operational readiness review meetings
- Support handoff documentation and service transition checklists
- Ongoing service review and improvement discussions

---

## Risk and Dependency Owners

### Role Summary
Risk and Dependency Owners track cross-team issues, blockers, and risks that can affect delivery outcomes. They make dependencies visible and ensure accountability for driving resolution paths and escalation decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain visibility into project risks, assumptions, and dependencies
- Identify and escalate blockers that require cross-functional decision-making
- Coordinate mitigation plans and ownership for key dependencies
- Support early warning and decision logs across teams
- Ensure risk implications are understood by project and executive stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Project Managers to maintain risk registers and mitigation plans
- Collaborate with Product Managers and Delivery Managers to assess impact on scope, timelines, or value delivery
- Support Sponsors by surfacing risk trends that require strategic intervention or resourcing decisions

### Typical Communication
- Risk review meetings and dependency tracking updates
- Escalation notes and mitigation planning discussions
- Stakeholder briefings on critical path risks and trade-offs

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify accountability, improve handoff quality, and make decision-making more effective across project delivery lifecycle stages.
